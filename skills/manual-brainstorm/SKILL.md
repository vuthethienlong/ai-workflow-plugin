# Manual brainstorm

## Inputs
Goal, prior summary/decisions/handover, current user answers, context access.

## Steps
1. Read existing brainstorm, decision, and handover artifacts first. If absent, say what history is unavailable; do not invent it.
2. Summarize settled decisions and open questions briefly; continue from the next unresolved point through focused user dialogue.
3. Record new options, rationale, assumptions, user decisions, and remaining questions in the [summary](../../templates/brainstorm-summary.md).
4. Stop at critical decisions/approval gates.

## Artifacts
Updated brainstorm summary and [task handover](../../templates/task-handover.md) when changing sessions.

## Stop and handoff
Stop when a critical user decision is required or goal is clarified. Continue to [idea-to-backlog](../idea-to-backlog/SKILL.md) or [issue-to-spec](../issue-to-spec/SKILL.md).

## Invocation
“Read \`ai-workflow/skills/manual-brainstorm/SKILL.md\` and the prior handover/artifacts for job [id]. Continue from the last unresolved question.”
